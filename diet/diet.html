Non-Negotiable Rules — All 8 Weeks

01
Weigh every morning, same conditions
After toilet, before eating or drinking. Log it. Use a 7-day rolling average — don't panic at single day fluctuations.
02
Treadmill is the floor, not the ceiling
45 min every day if a session is missed. If all sessions happen, treadmill on top. Sunday always has treadmill regardless.
03
Weigh your protein sources
Big portions are your stated saboteur. 250g steak is not "a steak". Weigh it for the first 3 weeks until your eye is calibrated.
04
Track in MyFitnessPal daily
Log cooking oils, butter, every gram. Hidden calories in butter and olive oil are where deficits disappear.
05
3L water minimum every day
You already do this — maintain it. Increase to 4L on training days. Water supports fat metabolism and hunger suppression.
06
Weekends are not off days
You've flagged weekends as your weak point. Social eating is fine — order steak, skip the chips. One bad meal won't kill a cut; one bad weekend will.
07
No yoghurt, fruit or dairy in W8
From Day 4 of Week 8: dairy out, fruit out. Every gram of carbohydrate holds water. You're preparing to watercut — keep it clinical.
08
Check in if stall hits at Week 4
If weight doesn't move for 7+ days at Week 4, report it before making changes. Likely water retention or tracking gaps — not a metabolic issue yet.
